Practical notes:
- Memory: 19M rows of pandas on 15 GB of RAM is tight. Convert the CSV to Parquet once. DuckDB or Polars would help, and so would modeling on stratified samples (for example 1–2M    rows), which loses little accuracy.
- Separate the sectors. Train models separately for credit reporting vs everything else, or include a bureau indicator. Otherwise the bureaus dominate everything the model learns. - Re-download with narratives if you can. The narrative text (consumers who complaints) opens up topic modeling, sentiment analysis and transformerclassification.
